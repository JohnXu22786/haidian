AI scenarios and task coverage (six domains + taskbook agent.1-6)

Five AI+ scenarios: city running digital twin AI, urban-form performance AI assessment, infrastructure load AI prediction, anonymized space-use sensing AI, public feedback AI aggregation. Only anonymized aggregated data is collected; personal biometric and trajectory information is not retained across scenarios; no always-on recognition devices are deployed; mass behavioural profiling is forbidden.

AI domains: AI space (cellular block / physical-virtual), AI governance (form assessment + time-responsive), AI industry (mixed function + reversible growth), AI public services (accessible guidance), AI culture (smart-pattern installation + open day), AI transport (time-responsive streets + route guidance), each with human-review fallback.

Three-tier scope

Coordinated research area: new urban form and regional innovation coordination relationship (directional prediction of coordination with along-belt universities, science-innovation sub-areas and international residence, official text caliber ~43.6 sq km); Overall design area: ~11.4 sq km (provisional concept caliber, cellular block layout); Key detailed design area: Cellular Block, Smart-Pattern Terrace, Growing Court three nodes (~368.4 ha, text caliber).

This package's sub-scope is an internal subset of the overall design area and the three key areas; all sub-scope items rely on the provisional boundary, and must not be escalated to an official redline.

Land-use structure (conceptual structural ratio)

The ratio in this page's land-use-structure figure uses the "conceptual structural ratio" as a caliber; the denominator is the "rough carrying surface within the overall design area", and the aggregation is the conceptual carrying-surface union, used only for form-structure illustration. metrics.json's green_ratio~0.11 belongs to a different caliber: "green-space area / overall-design-area geometric area", with a different denominator and aggregation. All specific areas, ratios, green ratio, public service ratio are based on the recompute version after official GIS/CAD geometry arrives.
