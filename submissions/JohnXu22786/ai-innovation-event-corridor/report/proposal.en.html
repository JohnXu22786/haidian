JINGZHANG AI INNOVATION EVENT CORRIDOR (RELAY·JZ)

Global AI Event System and Long-Term Operation (Concept)

1. Proposal Identity & Core Concept

  • Primary Name: "Jingzhang AI Relay Belt" (English: RELAY·JZ). "Relay" carries three connotations: railway heritage continuity, event content handovers, and developer generation synergy.
  • Vision: Transforming event hosting into long-term innovation ecosystem stewardship along the railway heritage green corridor.

2. Design Basis & Three-Level Scope Framework

  • Official Scopes: Coordinated Research Scope (~43.6 km²), Overall Design Scope (~11.41 km² / 11,412,825 sqm), Key Areas (368.4 ha).
  • Nested Strategy: Macro research defines synergy, master plan sets spatial framework, key nodes define user experience.

3. Regional Synergy & Official Two Wings Alignment

The proposal strictly adheres to the official Two Wings framework:

  • Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing (West Wing): Technology service interfaces, innovation capital matching, talent element allocation hub.
  • Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing (East Wing): Scenario open testbed, civic public experience, native commercial integration.
  • North-South Regional Synergy Interfaces (Supplementary): Connecting Future Science City & Huairou (North) and Beijing E-Town & Jing-Jin-Ji (South). Does not substitute the Two Wings.

4. Three Key Nodes Detailed Design

  • Node 1: Jingzhang Forum Plaza (North / Zhongzhiyuan): Open plaza themed on heritage railway platforms with retractable stands and main stage for summits, ceremonies and keynotes.
  • Node 2: Code Dock (Central / AI Origin Community): Developer camp space with flexible workstations, 24h hackathon pods, and algorithm sprint rooms.
  • Node 3: Loop Gallery (South / Dazhongsi): Linear pitch corridor along the green belt with convertible demo booths, micro-theatres, and investment matchmaking closing the 'Practice-Contest-Exhibit-Invest' cycle.

5. AI+ Scenarios & Safety Governance

Five AI+ scenarios operating strictly under anonymized aggregation and human review: 1. Smart Event Scheduling & Registration Aggregation; 2. Multilingual Live Captioning & Interpretation Assistance; 3. Coding Contest AI Co-pilot (Anonymized hints & post-contest review); 4. Anonymized Crowd Heat-Mapping & Safety Dispatch; 5. Accessible Multimodal Wayfinding (Universal navigation).

6. Land Use, Mobility & Single-Caliber Metrics

  • Single-Caliber Land Use: Parks & Green (28.23%), R&D (27.39%), Business/Finance (16.03%), Commercial (13.86%), Cultural (13.54%), Residential (0.95%). Total sum strictly 100.0%.
  • Green Ratio: green_ratio ≈ 11.64% (1,328,450 sqm / 11,412,825 sqm).
  • Public Space Ratio: public_space_ratio ≈ 0.45% (51,350 sqm / 11,412,825 sqm).
  • Slow-Traffic Greenway: ~10.15 km continuous green spine with transit shuttles.

7. Implementation Phasing & Annual Programs

  • Phasing: Near-term (1-3 yrs) greenway connection & 3 nodes delivery; Mid-term (3-5 yrs) surrounding renewal; Long-term (5-10 yrs) regional synergy.
  • Annual Programs: AI Developer Conference, Open-Source Co-Creation Week, Jingzhang Innovation Festival.
