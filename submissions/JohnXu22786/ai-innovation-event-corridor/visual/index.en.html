Overview map

Three scopes

Per the official announcement wording: coordinated research area ~43.6 km2; overall design area ~11.4 km2; key areas ~368.4 ha (Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster). This package's geometry is a conceptual sub-scope (provisional) under these official scopes; recompute when official geometry is published. The three positionings (culture belt / urban AI life experience belt / AI fusion belt) and five functions map to a "three districts & two wings synergy loop": three districts are the official key areas; two wings point north (Future Science City - Huairou Science City) and south (E-Town - Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei); the loop is "event flow -> district conversion -> community service -> talent return" (concept).

Key areas

Three nodes (concept): Jingzhang Forum Plaza (retractable stands, outdoor main stage, old-platform imagery); Code Dock (day workstations, night hackathons, modular cabins); Loop Gallery (convertible booths, mini-theatre, loop pitch circulation). All facilities are modular, reversible and demountable; final siting is subject to authority and heritage review. Twelve scenario cards and three test-verification pilots (protocols in proposal.md) form the scenario-space-operation matrix; the geometry layer holds 3 landmark nodes + 5 scenario nodes = 8 spatial nodes.

Land-use zones

Conceptual land-use structure (single caliber: union of all land_use.geojson features, EPSG:4548, rounded, recompute on official data): research ~27%; park & green ~28%; business & finance ~16%; commercial ~14%; cultural ~14%; residential ~1% (total 100%). Same source and caliber as the area metrics in metrics.json; neither current nor planned official indicators.

Slow traffic

Slow-traffic first: a continuous green-belt slow axis links the nodes, connecting public space rather than being severed by vehicles. Major event traffic relies on rail plus shuttles (shuttle routes, booked buses, shared-bike dispatch), with staged zone-by-zone egress and buffer zones; arrival is "rail station -> shuttle -> security -> venue". Rail context is schematic public information (Qinghe Station - Xizhimen direction); exact stations are governed by official publications.

Blue-green network

"Green belt as axis, node parks as beads": preserve existing tree belts and site texture, use rain gardens and permeable paving for ecological resilience; plazas, lawns and platform-like sites switch between venues, markets and daily leisure. Public-space component library (concept): common components (seating/lighting/signage/greening), event components (stages/stands/booths/cabins), service components (restrooms/medical/storage/accessibility aids) and mandatory event components (emergency lighting/evacuation signage). A "Jingzhang Honor" display system (concept) at Loop Gallery and Forum Plaza, published after public notice and joint review.

Buildings

Retain-renovate-demolish in parallel: retain the heritage green belt, station buildings and industrial structures of historical value; functionally renew inefficient factory and park buildings into event, office, exhibition and support space; demolish only scattered dilapidated buildings with demonstrated safety hazards, decided case by case. Conceptual building envelopes are displayed rounded in hectares with source, formula, confidence and recompute trigger; no scale conclusions.

Renewal projects

Three-phase renewal project list (concept): near term (1-3 yr) connect the green-belt skeleton, build the three nodes and launch the annual programme system (pilot: Code Dock; preconditions: compliance review, public notice, no-disturbance pledge, capacity thresholds); mid term (3-5 yr) functional renewal around nodes and stronger rail connectivity; long term (5-10 yr) corridor-wide linkage and north/south wing synergy. Annual programmes: AI Developer Conference, Open-Source Co-Creation Week, Jingzhang Innovation Festival (3 brands + monthly community events). The phasing and long-term operation matrix (advisory RACI roles, cost tiers, KPIs, human takeover, fail/pause thresholds, public feedback, annual review) and the developer-community conversion mechanism (compact, deliberation, points, notices, filing) are in proposal.md.

AI scenarios

Five AI+ scenario groups (smart scheduling & registration aggregation; multilingual captions & interpretation assist; AI co-pilot for coding contests; anonymized crowd heat & safety dispatch; accessible multimodal wayfinding) are laid out as 12 scenario cards. Technical protocols cover model evaluation, data quality, error stratification and runtime monitoring. Boundary: anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance, no individual-identifying tracking; personal and trajectory data never persist across scenarios; no always-on identification devices.
