Overall Map

One Loop, Three Nodes: the Night Loop organises a continuous night slow route along the Jingzhang heritage park belt and transit stops (concept locations), connecting Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi; lighting is tiered gateway - path - node - dark zone. All geometry comes from this package's geometry/*.geojson (provisional, EPSG:4548 plot).

Transport & Slow Mobility

Integrated night-return slow lighting and safe routes: lighting tiered gateway - path - node - dark zone, one pole serving path lighting, night signage and help-point marking; last-train connection is a slow-guidance concept mechanism (not a schedule commitment); municipal facilities are reversible point components.

Blue-Green & Public Space

"Green belt as axis, node parks as beads"; dark-sky friendly low-brightness tiers and spill-light control around green and water edges, night lighting avoiding green core sensitive zones; the Night Loop shares seating, shade and accessible facilities with the daytime public-space network.

Buildings

Concept building layer (geometry/buildings.geojson): 12 retain-first concept envelopes (approx. 11.2 ha), reversible and low-footprint; no large new buildings for the night economy; FAR, building height and similar official-condition indicators remain unknown.

Renewal Projects

Three concept lists (not investment commitments): lighting & environment (light-environment tier demonstration, night-return path lighting, light-pollution monitoring pilot, dark-sky zones); performance & market (Night Stage reversible components, Night Market stall kits, guidance and filing templates); operation & governance (time permits, service credits, annual disclosure, resident opinion channel). Phasing: near-term 1-3y / mid-term 3-5y / long-term 5-10y, each phase with evaluation and stop conditions; pilot and long-term operation follow a concept RACI, pre-admission conditions, monitoring baseline (unmeasured items marked "to be established") and stop-and-withdrawal procedure.
