JINGZHANG MASTER CONCEPT (MASTER·JZ): Belt-Level Overall Concept and Functional Coordination

This page is the static visualisation index of the “JINGZHANG MASTER CONCEPT (京章智轴)” concept package: driven by self-drawn diagrams, GeoJSON, metrics.json and the persisted self-check. It covers overview map, three-level scope, key areas, land-use zones, slow mobility, blue-green network, buildings, renewal projects, AI scenarios, core metrics, task coverage, self-check status, sources and assumptions. Everything is concept suggestion / reference proposal on a provisional boundary; recompute after official data release.

Overview Map

OVERVIEW MAP: official scope hierarchy (taskbook text caliber): coordinated research area about 43.6 km2, overall design area about 11.4 km2, key areas about 368.4 ha. This package sits beneath those tiers on provisional geometry: Overview Hub (south) - Weave-Core Court (middle) - Green-Link Ring (north), north at top matching the figures; the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing (west) and the Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing (east) are stitched across the corridor.

Three-Level Scope

THREE-LEVEL SCOPE: coordinated research area - belt-and-city coordination and regional synergy (Zhongguancun / Wudaokou / Xueyuan Road / Shangdi / Qinghe / Beiwai Community / Future Science City / Huairou Science City / E-Town / Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei); overall design area - three-zone/two-wing structure and regulatory-plan-level urban design guideline directions; key areas - detailed design of the three nodes.

Key Areas

KEY AREAS: Overview Hub (Digital Memory Screen, operations centre, honor wall), Weave-Core Court (JZ Cloud Bridge, community services, multilingual guiding), Green-Link Ring (Blue-Green Sensing Ring, slow-traffic interchange, reversible components). All siting is concept-level and must be re-validated through planning, heritage and authority assessment.

Land-Use Zones

LAND USE: 24 concept blocks (national land-use classification); retain-renovate-demolish together: retain remains and texture, renovate by function insertion, demolish only after case-by-case demonstration; the concept parkland share about 33% (node caliber) and the green ratio about 11.6% (overall caliber) are explicitly different and recomputed on official area release.

Slow Mobility

SLOW MOBILITY: continuous green-belt slow-mobility spine linking the three nodes, east-west cross-corridor stitching, time-phased event dispersal; station-anchored with shuttle reservations; 15-minute living circles with continuous accessible routes, all-age, age-friendly and child-friendly elements in paving, lighting, signage and facility scale.

Blue-Green Network

BLUE-GREEN NETWORK: “green belt as axis, node parks as beads” forming a continuous network; cultural devices light, transparent and reversible; the cultural narrative in three movements - Jingzhang railway memory, Zhongguancun innovation spirit, AI new-culture space; the wayfinding-signage subsystem sits under the belt-wide brand system.

Buildings

BUILDINGS: existing stock is prioritised for reuse-first conversion to innovation exchange and community services; no FAR, height or retain-demolish conclusions are made.

Renewal Projects

RENEWAL: four project categories (coordination platform / slow-axis connection / stock insertion / blue-green weaving); phasing near 1-3 / mid 3-5 / far 5-10 years; every phase defines lead/collaborator roles (RACI-like), preconditions, decision gates and pilot exit conditions; cost tiers are qualitative only (low/medium/high).

AI Scenarios

AI SCENARIOS: AI governance in three sentences - anonymized aggregates only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance. Scenario cards S-01..S-10 cover industry, space, transport, public services, culture and governance; test-validation scenarios T-01..T-03; technical protocols in four steps - model evaluation, data quality, error stratification, runtime monitoring.

Task Coverage

TASK COVERAGE: compliance_matrix.json links taskbook sections 1.3-1.5 and agent.1-6 item by item: agent.1 overall concept and functional coordination; agent.2 AI innovation ecosystem atlas and global cases; agent.3 scenario cards and test validation; agent.4 AI public space, landmarks, honor display and component library; agent.5 cultural narrative and brand; agent.6 programmes and long-term operation.

Sources

SOURCES: sources.json contains only verifiable entries (publisher, page URL, published and accessed dates, licence and reuse boundary): announcement, taskbook, national standards, provisional geometry, 8 benchmark cases and asset entries for fonts, logo and figures; no official data is fabricated.

Assumptions

ASSUMPTIONS: assumptions.json registers six concept assumptions: provisional boundary, missing statutory controls, data boundary, design direction, privacy boundary and advisory operation; all are concept caliber and recomputed on official data.

Self-Check Status

SELF-CHECK: self_check.json persists the four trusted gates (deterministic / spatial / visual / professional) and the package figure QC (ink and clip measurements; text overlap is a generation-time layout check, not machine-asserted post-hoc).
