## Scheme Name and Overall Concept (Concept)

The overall concept is the **Jingzhang Master Concept** (MASTER·JZ — Metropolitan Axis of Smart Technology, Ecology & Rail): an axis-level concept built on the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park green belt, connecting memory, innovation and liveability; a necklace-like structure links stations and districts, and a coordination loop along the green belt organises the circulation of industry, academia, city and people. The primary name "JINGZHANG" ("京章") is a homophone of Jing-Zhang (JZ), carrying the dual meaning of "chapter" and "seal": the Jingzhang Railway opened the chapter of China's own railway engineering, Haidian writes today's innovation chapter, and the belt runs through the city like a long seal. The unified motif system uses Chinese character families (hub, court, ring, chapter) in parallel with JZ codes (JZ-01...); the three key nodes are named "总览枢" (Overview Hub), "织核庭" (Weave-Core Court) and "绿链环" (Green-Link Ring), and annual programmes carry the "JINGZHANG+" family prefix.

**Three positioning pillars**: 1) public activation axis of century-old railway memory; 2) exchange and showcase axis of global AI innovation coordination; 3) future demonstration axis of blue-green slow-traffic liveability. **Five functions**: 1) cultural memory and heritage display; 2) innovation exchange and launch display; 3) commercial services and daily amenities; 4) blue-green ecology and recreation; 5) transit connection and slow-traffic interchange.

**Explicit mapping to the taskbook spatial units**: the north culture-memory zone responds to the northward extension of the AI Origin Community (Qinghe—Qinghuadong Road section); the middle innovation-vitality zone anchors the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Zone (Wudaokou—Xueyuan Road section) and serves the Beijing AI Origin Community; the south eco-liveability zone links the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Zone (Zhichun Road—Xizhimen section); the two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing (west; Zhongguancun West—Shangdi innovation blocks) and the Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing (east; Xueyuan Road—Xiaoyue River university blocks). The loop uses the green belt as its axis, stations as valves and districts as vessels: west-wing industry innovation, exchange in the green belt and stations, east-wing university incubation feeding back to the west — a closed "industry-academia-city-people" loop.

**Official scope hierarchy and the position of this package**: the announcement text states the coordinated research area at roughly 43.6 km², the overall design area at roughly 11.4 km², and the key areas at roughly 368.4 ha. All geometry and areas in this package are provisional concept indications beneath those official levels; everything is recomputed when official GIS/CAD data is released.

**Regional synergy**: the belt forms a radial synergy network with Zhongguancun, Wudaokou, Xueyuan Road, Shangdi and Qinghe, extending toward Beiwai Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Technological Development Area and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region — all concept-level judgements.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Industry research identifies the belt's "coordination" effect: the green-belt axis organises the needs of universities, innovation blocks, internationalised residential areas and functional parks into a two-way accessible coordination loop, forming a "belt—district—block" three-tier structure. The AI innovation chain "basic research—technology transfer—enterprise application—public experience" is located along the corridor, positioning the belt as the "urban infrastructure" for innovation-factor flows. The mechanism is expressed as an eight-factor ecosystem atlas — **land** (renewal and functional conversion), **space** (green belt and station exchange spaces), **industry** (acceleration zone, cluster zone and service wing in division of labour), **capital** (multi-stakeholder co-construction and foundation models), **talent** (university pipelines and developer community), **compute** (shared along-corridor compute facilities as a proposal), **data** (anonymized aggregated open-data catalogue as a proposal), and **scenario** (scenario opening and test validation); all are directional mechanism suggestions. Future-city directions: industrial community-isation, spatial compounding, green low-carbon transition and digital-physical integration, with resilience and flexibility. Regional synergy extends to Beiwai Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Technological Development Area and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region — concept judgements only, implying no land or investment commitment.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design organises the "one axis, three zones, multiple beads, two wings, one loop" structure with the green belt as its skeleton, explicitly mapped to the taskbook units: culture-memory zone (AI Origin Community extension), innovation-vitality zone (Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Zone), eco-liveability zone (linking the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Zone), the Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario-Empowerment Wing; dominant functions and compatibility directions are defined by segment, and renewal is a retain-renovate-demolish concept caliber. At regulatory depth the package outputs urban design guideline directions (interface, massing relations, slow traffic, blue-green baseline, character) without concluding FAR or height values. **East-west stitching and north-south linkage**: the two wings are stitched across the corridor (bridges, underpasses, junction works), while the north-south green-belt slow axis links the three nodes; stitching and gap-breaking items are managed in a rolling project list. **Dazhongsi AI-native business formats (concept)**: AI-native retail pop-ups, multilingual intelligent wayfinding, digital-art exhibitions and data-visual dining concepts, all running on anonymized aggregates with human review and reversible lightweight facilities; no floor area or ridership figures are concluded.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three original nodes run south to north: the **Overview Hub** (south gateway, Xizhimen—Zhichun Road direction: belt gateway, coordination display and the narrative start of railway history), the **Weave-Core Court** (middle, Wudaokou—Xueyuan Road: composite function and community-service node interlacing station, university and community services around courtyard spaces), and the **Green-Link Ring** (north, where the green belt meets the transverse green corridor: slow-traffic interchange and blue-green system conversion hub). All facility siting is conceptual and must be re-validated through planning, heritage and authority assessment. Node plans and sections are expressed in the drawing set and geometry layers, supported by a **reversible component library** (lightweight canopies, demountable display frames, modular seating planters, temporary paving — light, transparent, reversible) and an **honor display system** (an honor-wall/achievement-screen direction publicly recognising developers, volunteers, co-building enterprises and residents, on anonymized aggregates, never individual identification). AI public-space design is led by three AI landmarks: the "Digital Memory Screen" at the Overview Hub (history and operation visualisation, human-reviewed publication), the "JZ Cloud Bridge" at the Weave-Core Court (cross-corridor stitching and exchange interface), and the "Blue-Green Sensing Ring" at the Green-Link Ring (reversible device sensing microclimate and greenery).

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Retain-renovate-demolish proceed together as a concept caliber: **retain** railway remains, historic buildings and block texture; **renovate** by functional insertion and spatial upgrading of existing buildings, prioritising innovation exchange and community services; **demolish** only after case-by-case demonstration of no preservation value, following statutory procedure. All areas are concept caliber; no FAR, building scale or demolition quantities are concluded and no engineering-feasibility conclusions are given. **Caliber difference note (recompute rule)**: the "about 33% concept parkland" refers to the recommended green open-space envelopes within the three key areas as a share of node concept ranges (provisional geometry aggregate), which differs in caliber from the "green ratio about 11.6%" (green polygon area over the concept overall-design-area polygon, also a provisional geometry aggregate); the two must not be mixed. The package maintains an area ledger on the official text caliber and recomputes everything under the same aggregation rule when official areas and redlines are released. Costs are expressed only as qualitative tiers (low/medium/high) with estimation method and recompute trigger, never precise amounts.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Slow traffic first: existing rail and bus stations anchor the system (no new-line proposals); the continuous green-belt slow axis links the Overview Hub, Weave-Core Court and Green-Link Ring from south to north; the east and west wings are stitched across the corridor (bridges, underpasses, junction works) to connect university blocks and campuses — the east-west stitching strategy; shuttle services and shared-bike marshalling areas are reserved; event traffic relies on rail plus shuttles with time-phased dispersal, reducing car dependence. North-south linkage is completed by the green-belt slow axis and the Green-Link Ring conversion. Municipal works follow intensive sharing: corridor-aligned utilities, undergrounding and sponge layouts in the green-belt section are directional suggestions. Public services are anchored at stations — community services, culture, health and convenience facilities forming 15-minute living circles. Following the Barrier-Free Environment Law article 39 boundary (on-site guidance and human-operated service apply strictly to the enumerated public-service venues: medical, social security, financial, living-payment services; not generalised to every space or digital interface), the package proposes continuous accessible routes, information alternatives, offline human channels and a participation-feedback loop; all-age, age-friendly and child-friendly requirements are delivered through paving, lighting, signage and facility-scale elements. AI-assisted decisions always carry human review.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green public-space system uses the "green belt as axis, node parks as beads": the heritage-park green belt forms the blue-green skeleton linking pocket green spaces and open space into a continuous network; cultural devices integrate with parks lightly, transparently and reversibly, never blocking heritage remains or sightlines. The guidance and interpretation system is restrained and unified, acting as the **wayfinding and signage subsystem** under the belt-wide brand system, not mixed with the overall logo system. The **cultural narrative** unfolds in three movements — "Jingzhang railway memory, Zhongguancun innovation spirit, AI new-culture space": station vocabulary and rusted colour tell the history chapter, campuses and innovation faces tell the innovation chapter, digital art and interactive exhibitions tell the future chapter; the narrative copy is mostly Chinese with English summaries for international communication. Character control is restrained and unified: colour, materials, signage and street furniture follow one motif; night lighting, public art and the guide system follow the same layout language without specifying styles; shade, sponge and noise reduction improve comfort, with all-age and accessibility elements included.
