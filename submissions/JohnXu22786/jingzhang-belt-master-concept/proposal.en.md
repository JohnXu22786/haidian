## Outline
- Jingzhang Master Concept (MASTER.JZ): using the Jingzhang heritage green belt as the axis to coordinate the belt's overall concept - heritage memory, innovation vitality and ecological living as the three positioning pillars.
- Three-zone/two-wing coordination loop: culture-memory zone, innovation-vitality zone and eco-living zone woven along the axis, with east and west flanking districts stitched across the corridor.
- Node 1 - Overview Hub: belt-level gateway and coordination display node (naming system, logo direction, overall structure diagram).
- Node 2 - Weave-Core Court: functional composite node where culture, innovation and community services interlock.
- Node 3 - Green-Link Ring: transfer hub node between the slow-traffic axis and the blue-green open space network.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (overall operation situation AI visualisation, function-fit AI assessment, green-belt slow-traffic flow AI prediction, blue-green space sensing AI, public participation opinion AI aggregation); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; east-west crossing stitching; event traffic organised around rail transit plus shuttles; annual events: belt master open day, functional coordination workshop, slow-green experience season.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
