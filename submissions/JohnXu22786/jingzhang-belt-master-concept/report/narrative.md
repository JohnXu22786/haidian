This narrative summarizes the structured AI package (jingzhang-belt-master-concept, v1.1, round-1 repair).

**Honesty contract**: everything is concept suggestion; no FAR, height, retain-demolish, investment, ridership or capacity conclusions; boundaries are provisional and recomputed after official data release; organizer-side data gaps are annotated, not fabricated; an asset-level rights ledger (cases, fonts, maps, icons, code, names, logo and generative assets) is registered in sources.json.

**Deliverables**: proposal.md + proposal.en.md (substantively equivalent bilingual pair, front-matter contract v1), report/proposal.html + report/proposal.en.html (rendered offline, embedded NotoSansSC data-URI font), visual/index.html + visual/index.en.html, drawings (A0 two boards + A3 six-page booklet in zh and en), five canonical figures plus ecosystem atlas and logo draft in zh/en, geometry layers, metrics.json, matrices (compliance/standard/design-depth), risk.json, assumptions.json, sources.json and self_check.json.
