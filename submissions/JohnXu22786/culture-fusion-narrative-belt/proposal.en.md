**English convenience summary** (full binding narrative is the Chinese proposal.md; this summary may lag the zh text).

以「京源新叙（FUSION·JZ · 百年京张·中关村·AI新文化融合叙事带）」沿京张铁路原线绿带与遗址公园组织三源文化融合叙事的概念：把京张铁路历史文化资源系统、中关村创新文化与AI新文化转译为一条可阅读、可体验、可共创的空间故事线，「一带三馆」——三源叙事馆（三条叙事线交汇的常设展陈馆）、跨界对话廊（三文化交融的线性展廊与装置带）、新文化策展舱（公众策展与AI生成内容策展空间、人工复核发布），五类AI+场景（文化叙事数据AI整理与溯源标注、馆藏内容多语AI讲解、文化符号一致性校验AI、策展内容AI辅助生成且人工复核发布、观众匿名动线与反馈聚合）仅处理匿名聚合数据、关键决策人工复核、禁止过度监控，交通慢行优先、绿带连续慢行轴贯通节点，分期实施+年度活动体系（三源文化季、跨界对话周、新文化策展日）。全部为概念建议、参考方案，基于 provisional 边界，官方数据发布后复算。

## Outline
- Jingyuan New Narrative (FUSION.JZ): using the Jingzhang heritage green belt as the backbone to turn the Jingzhang railway heritage, Zhongguancun innovation culture and AI new culture into a readable, experiential, co-creatable spatial storyline.
- Node 1 - Three-Source Narrative Pavilion: permanent exhibition pavilion where the three narrative lines (railway heritage, innovation spirit, AI culture) meet and interweave.
- Node 2 - Cross-culture Dialogue Gallery: linear gallery and installation belt where the three cultures converse, with renewable narrative points along the green belt.
- Node 3 - New Culture Curation Pod: public curation and AI-assisted content curation space; all AI-generated content is human-reviewed before publication.
- 5 AI+ scenarios (AI-assisted narrative-data organisation with source tracing, multilingual AI interpretation of collection content, cultural-symbol consistency checking AI, AI-assisted curation that is human-reviewed before release, anonymized visitor movement and feedback aggregation); anonymized aggregation only, key decisions human-reviewed, no over-surveillance.
- Slow-traffic-first: a continuous slow-traffic axis along the green belt links the three nodes; event traffic organised around rail transit plus shuttles; annual events: tri-source culture season, cross-culture dialogue week, new culture curation day.
- All figures are concept suggestions on a provisional boundary; recompute when official data is published.
